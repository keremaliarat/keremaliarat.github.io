KAASHIF

Yürüyüş, koşu ve bisiklet aktivitelerini haritada gösterir. Gezdiğin yerlerin "Savaş Sisi"ni açar, rotalarını 3B arazi üzerinde canlı telemetriyle yeniden oynatır ve paylaşılabilir video üretir. Apps

KAASHIF

Shows your walks, runs and rides on a map. It clears the "Fog of War" over the places you have explored, replays your routes on 3D terrain with live telemetry and turns them into shareable videos. Record activities in the app, import GPX / FIT / TCX files, or read them from Health Connect / Apple Health.

Android and iOS · Free · Only ad: a short rewarded ad before generating a video · Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store
